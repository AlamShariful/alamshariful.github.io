About Me

Self-motivated, goal-oriented security researcher and enthusiastic polyglot programmer. Ph.D. graduate in Computing with an emphasis in Cybersecurity.

I completed my Ph.D. in Computing with a focus on Cybersecurity from Boise State University in May 2025. Currently, I work as a Senior Security & Crypto Engineer at Marvell Technology in Santa Clara, California. My research interests are mainly in cybersecurity with particular emphasis on operating system security and hardware security. My doctoral work investigated covert channels in modern CPU architectures.

I was born and raised in Dhaka, the capital of Bangladesh. I earned my Bachelor's degree in Computer Science and Engineering in Bangladesh in 2014 and obtained my Master's degree in Computer Science from Lamar University, Texas, in the fall of 2018. In addition to my research interests, I enjoy exploring new places and spending my free time chatting with family and friends.
